\section{Dissertation}
\textbf{Title:} Essays on Medical Debt and Quality of Care\\
\textbf{Committee:} Hector P. Rodriguez [Chair], Ambar La Forgia, Becky Staiger, Charleen Hsuan (Penn State) \\
\textbf{Short Description:} I consider affordability and commercialization in the United States and policy remedies to protect patient welfare. In my first paper, I leverage borrower-level estimates of race and ethnicity to assess disparities in medical debt in collections. In my second paper, I assess the impact of state-implemented surprise billing laws before the No Surprises Act on medical debt. In my third paper, I analyze how hospital market consolidation produces heterogeneous impacts on patient access and quality of care by a) race and ethnicity and b) payer status.
